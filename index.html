Model & assumptions

The forecast anchors at your starting BG and projects forward only — the meter reading already contains everything that happened before it.

Insulin is the live on-board number you read off the Omnipod 5. The remaining drop is ISF x IOB, spread evenly over the "insulin action ahead" window. Real insulin is front-loaded, so the depth of the low is solid but its timing is approximate.

Glucose & food start only after an onset lag (default 15 min, matching Lynette's real-world delay), then add a BG credit as they absorb (fast for tablets, slower for food) that persists — it doesn't fade on its own. What pulls it back down is the insulin and exercise, so adding glucose lifts the whole forward curve, the trough included, by offsetting the insulin one-for-one.

Exercising adds a steady BG drain for as long as it runs. Set an intended duration, or leave it at 0 (open) and toggle off when you stop.

Predicted low is the lowest point of the forward curve. It's a planning estimate, not a guarantee — verify with a reading, especially before deciding not to treat.
